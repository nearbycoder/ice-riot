Features

All riot.

Fast 3-on-3 on a tight arcade rink with turbo, flaming pucks and fights. It plays like the 90s arcade hockey cabinets and their console ports, with smarter AI and a lot more players.

8-player local multiplayer

Two players share the keyboard and up to eight gamepads plug in, in any mix. Pick a side on the join screen. With 7 or 8 humans the game switches to 4-on-4 automatically.

One-timers & give-and-go

Shoot + Pass sends it and rips the return the moment it arrives. You can call for the puck from CPU teammates too.

Instant replays

Every goal gets the horn, the goal light, a close-up of the scorer and a cinematic replay. You can skip it if you're impatient.

Arcade or Pro rules

Arcade is anything goes. Pro adds icing, offsides, hooking and tripping penalties, and power plays.

Tournament mode

An arcade ladder: beat every team in the league. If you lose, you replay the same team until you get past them.

5 difficulties

Very Easy to Very Hard, with CPU AI that actually scales. Comeback catch-up is optional.

Loud announcer

Scouting report

How the classics played

Before writing any code we went back to the source material: the instruction manuals and reviews of the 90s arcade hockey cabinets and their console ports. Here's what we kept, and what we fixed.

  1. The cabinets

    Three skaters and a goalie per side on a smaller arcade rink. No offsides, no icing, no penalties, just speed.

    ICE RIOT

    3-on-3 with CPU goalies and Arcade rules by default. Pro rules are a menu toggle away.

  2. The cabinets

    Turbo showed up as a colored ring under your skater's feet and recharged on its own.

    ICE RIOT

    Your player ring is also your turbo gauge. Turbo gives +45% top speed, sharper turns and harder hits, and it refills after a short breather.

  3. The cabinets

    Shoot + Turbo was the power shot: a fireball that could knock the goalie back into the net.

    ICE RIOT

    A flaming puck with a streak behind it. On a goal the whole net bursts into flames. It costs half your turbo.

  4. The cabinets

    A big enough check sent a player flying, and an ambulance drove across the top of the screen.

    ICE RIOT

    The victim gets launched, stick and helmet pop off, the screen shakes and the glass rattles. Yes, the ambulance is in.

  5. The cabinets

    Anger meters boiled over into fights with jabs, punches, grabs and uppercuts. The loser left the ice.

    ICE RIOT

    Health bars, four attacks and a block. The loser sits out the play and the winner's team gets full turbo and RAGE.

  6. The cabinets

    Four-player multiplayer was the big selling point.

    ICE RIOT

    We doubled it to eight: two on one keyboard and up to eight gamepads, in any mix.

  7. What critics said

    The single-player AI was weak and the commentary got repetitive fast.

    Fixed

    The AI is smarter and scales across five difficulties, and the announcer has 68 lines instead of a handful.

Under the hood

Every asset
is code

The repo has no hand-made art and no sample packs. Scripts build every model, texture, animation and sound, and the whole thing is open source.

  1. 01

    Procedural art in Blender

    Python scripts build the rink, the arena and its crowd, the skaters and goalies, 48 animation clips, and every crest and banner. Run them again and you get the same assets.

  2. 02

    Every sound synthesized

    173 sounds made with numpy DSP: Karplus–Strong guitars, FM e-piano, a drawbar organ and a synthesized rock kit, plus 5 original music tracks and an announcer run through an arena-PA chain.

  3. 03

    Unreal Engine 5.9 & C++

    Gameplay, AI, the six cameras, replays and the HUD are all written in C++.

  4. 04

    Ships to Mac & the web

    There's a native macOS build, plus Unreal's WebAssembly/WebGPU export so you can play in a browser tab.

# regenerate everything from source $ blender -b -P Blender/gen_arena.py $ python Tools/gen_audio.py # 173 WAVs, verified $ Tools/package.sh mac # → IceRiot.app $ Tools/package.sh web # → play/ (Wasm + WebGPU)
